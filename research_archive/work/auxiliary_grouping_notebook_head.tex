\begin{abstract}
An independent fixed grouping scale removes the unverified
large-block decay premise in the inherited-interaction norm
test. Physical averaging and its pivot chart keep their
factor $L$. We carry quantum activities on current $J$-blocks
and group Gaussian variables on current $JL$-blocks. After
physical rescaling these return to next-current $J$-blocks.
Known positive fixed-$L$ spatial decay becomes any prescribed
finite decay exponent in the grouped graph when $J$ is large.
The classical support expansions supply the finite cardinality
reserves required for that regrouping. The tunable Gaussian
density estimate then gives a fixed output support exponent,
and both the new correction and inherited background term
can return to the same positive carrier reserve.
An explicit order of finite parameter choices satisfies the
next-step support inequality. Analytic radii and norm constants
remain explicit losses, so this closes support-weight
compatibility without asserting a stable quantum induction.
\end{abstract}

Notebook 24 isolated a sufficient inequality that its original
cell convention could not verify from positivity of fixed-$L$
decay alone. Here the physical renormalization map, integration
variables and sharp cutoff remain the same; the independent
grouping changes how the support estimates are organized.
The result applies to the homogeneous small-field step on a
compatible cofinal family of periodic regulators.
