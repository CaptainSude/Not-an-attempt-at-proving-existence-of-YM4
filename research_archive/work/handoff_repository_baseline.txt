Draft, abstract and Sections 1.1–1.4, pp.1–23.

The object is the Wilson lattice measure for SU(2), on compatible periodic tori, and
correlations of one local gauge-invariant observable: smeared action density. Supports
of the test functions are pairwise disjoint. The density has canonical dimension four;
its leading separated two-point kernel has singularity |x-y|^-8. There is no unmentioned
prescription extending it across coincidence.

In the draft's convention,

A(U) = sum_p [1 - Re tr U(boundary p)],
dmu(U) is proportional to exp[-g_0^(-2) A(U)] dU, and tr(1) = 1.

The observable is the corresponding test-weighted plaquette sum. Complex sources change
action weights; connected correlations are derivatives of the logarithm of the sourced
partition function, and centred moments are assembled from cumulants. This convention
matters when comparing beta coefficients and two-point amplitudes. See Draft pp.7–9 and
I, Chapter 1, beginning at line 401.

Theorem 0: scope of the clauses.

This is a navigation map, not a substitute for the parameter hypotheses in the displayed
theorem.

- (0), running coupling. The draft asserts an actual recursively generated trajectory
stopped at a first-passage scale, with two-sided cumulative inverse-coupling bounds and,
for SU(2), a cumulative one-loop law. It does not require a positive decrement at every
step. I:2533–2554 explicitly distinguishes cumulative drift from a single-step sign.

- (i), ultraviolet stability. The application of Balaban's complete-density theorem uses
the weak-coupling containment furnished by (0). Stability concerns effective densities
and their prescribed representation, not by itself a continuum observable limit.

- (iii), sourced correlations. Connected action-density bounds are asserted uniformly in
cutoff; a separate per-ball estimate supplies the needed volume independence. This
source theorem and its running shift are substantive inputs, not consequences of a
vacuum partition-function estimate alone.

- (ii), limits and reconstruction. The claim includes torus and R^4 limits with the
stated fixed-order regularity, translations, hypercubic symmetry, permutation symmetry
and reflection positivity. It reconstructs a Hilbert space, vacuum and nonnegative
time-translation generator in the stated sense. The R^4 limit is subsequential.
Fixed-torus convergence without subsequences and identification of first-passage limits
use (v), inheriting its dependencies.

- (iv), a two-point witness. At small separated distances the connected two-point
function is comparable to the specified positive free curvature kernel times the fourth
power of a running coupling, with controlled relative error. A one-torus witness is
transferred to other volumes. Its consequences include a nonzero centred reflected
vector and logarithmic short-distance behavior.

- (v), a torus family. Under comparison inputs, a unique analytic one-parameter family
is asserted at fixed torus size. Two-cutoff and first-passage/tuned identifications are
stronger than subsequential compactness. They are not infinite-volume uniqueness or
clustering.

- (vi), rotations. Full O(4) invariance is conditional on a displayed far-sphere
angular-momentum-flux hypothesis, which the draft does not establish. Short-distance
approximate rotation statements and the README's later reflection argument are not
interchangeable assertions.

CORRECTIONS AND SUPERSEDED ROUTES

Historical notes remain for traceability. Their next-task and candidate language is
chronological. Later repairs and counterexamples take precedence. The following changes
are particularly important for a reviewer.

- No weakened baseline. The repository did claim R^4 existence. Its definition already
excluded a two-point inference of non-Gaussian interaction. Our additions must not be
presented against a fabricated weaker starting claim.

- The mandatory three-map source obstruction was withdrawn. An early reading of Raw II
Section 5.6 treated three localized response maps as the next unavoidable obstruction.
Raw I:73545, 4.21:rem-global-chain, uses four global carriers instead. The correction is
in work/source_stability_research.md, opening and Section 9. The conditional local
estimates remain; their asserted necessity does not. The two descriptions still need an
authoritative use-by-use reconciliation.

- Sharp windows were kept distinct. The retained-link cutoff and additional pivot
indicators cannot be conflated. Primary CMP116 p.12 excludes selected pivots from its
retained bond set. The nonlinear argument is Phi_V(gxi), not generally gPhi_V(xi). A bad
link implies Euclidean cell norm greater than the same threshold. The separate
good-window inclusion with a square-root dimension factor cannot improve the bad-event
tail.

- Tails are not localized logarithms. Joint Gaussian bad-block bounds alone do not give
connected log activities. Forest/cluster and strict-support arguments are additional
work. Grouping arbitrary large overlapping supports into Gaussian blocks can lose degree
summability; no routine extension claim substitutes for the actual
overlapping-interaction proof.

- Norm and radius losses were real. A fixed cardinality reserve does not pay all Taylor
coefficients for free. Transport uses the exact product Cauchy cost, common smaller
domains and a geometry budget. Auxiliary grouping/pre-gauge transport address these
losses rather than rename them. When auxiliary vertices disappear, connected-hull
estimates use Steiner length. Conversion to a terminal metric tree spends a factor of at
most two in the exponent.

- Positive norm, positive energy and a gap differ. A nonzero reflected vector can have a
zero-energy component. A time correlation ratio forces positive energy but no gap. A
constrained Gaussian gap cK^-2 is not a Yang–Mills Hamiltonian mass gap. Independent
random constants and generalized-free examples remain valid continuum-interface
cautions.

- Pressure and free boundaries did not identify the same symmetric state. Surface free
energy does not determine local states, and translation averaging need not preserve
positivity at a fixed mirror. The reflection proof therefore uses actual gluing and
comparison with a common-limit obligation, not unrelated states chosen for individual
tests.

- Gauges and Hessians needed exact corrections. The source scalar projection is not
automatically projection onto ker Q_0; a constrained biharmonic construction was
required. Ordinary and left-exponential Hessians differ by current curvature. Constraint
elimination adds multiplier curvature. Dropping either changes the determinant.

- Pinned and unpinned infrared factors cannot be exchanged. Point pinning can give a
scalar gap of order K^-4, not K^-2, and its long-time logarithm differs. A globally
based scalar factor has a constant-holonomy anomaly with forced vector compensation. The
later mean-zero pair is a different exact decomposition, not proof that the earlier
factors separately had the desired coefficient.

- A false difference-square formula was replaced. Zero row sums alone do not give the
naive component-valued common difference-square identity. Component-skew/odd terms
obstruct it. The corrected proof uses ordered Laurent factorization with zero constant
and first moments. The amended covariant Hessian and soft-vector/tail modules are
operative.

- Separate uniform relative vertices were not automatic. Pure-gauge and
hierarchy-interface examples give growing relative vertices while full heat traces
remain invariant. Current arguments use covariant densities, localized forms and
combined identities rather than the rejected componentwise bound.

- Affine probes need a total-kernel interpretation. A polynomial Cartan probe is not a
bounded periodic background. Its use needs localized Taylor kernels and polynomial
moments. A convenient local density on an affine field need not equal the total Maxwell
moment. Reanchoring, covariance and the limiting order are indispensable.

- Infinite kernels are not arbitrary finite kernels. Large-K magnetic/Bloch arguments
use a jointly chosen infinite structural system or explicit periodizations. Finite
winding errors and actual coefficient transfer are separate. Finite determinant algebra
alone does not justify thermodynamic derivative interchange.

- Full-measure shortcuts fail. Small plaquettes on a simply connected box give a
tree-gauge link bound proportional to a filling area. Alternating small curvature rules
out an automatic mesh-uniform physical C^1,alpha chart. Flat torus holonomies obstruct
an arbitrarily small global potential. Raw link marginals are Haar by gauge invariance,
so raw near-identity link probability is the wrong event.

REMAINING EXISTENCE INTERFACES AND AUDIT PRIORITIES

The local positive drift changes the next research task; it does not erase the remaining
implications.

Full densities and normalized large-field replacement.

One needs a full match to the CMP119/CMP122 class or a direct replacement theorem for
the chosen composite map. It must handle relevant bad components, exterior data, holes,
retained terms, interrupted operations and ages, with numerator/denominator cancellation
and summable bounds. The weak couplings of our local recursion are not automatically the
coefficients of that full recursion. The averaging axioms may permit the map, but all
used variational/domain hypotheses still require verification.

The new normalized fixed-box replacement is a useful concrete piece of this problem. It
does not identify fixed-boundary conditionals with conditionals on nonlinear block
averages, or establish iteration on post-RG densities. An exact all-history identity is
not a uniform norm bound on those histories.

Observables and a common continuum limit.

An external observable source changes full densities, counterterms and large-field
operations. Its cutoff/volume uniform control is not implied by holomorphy in a small
background connection. Analytic activity compactness is not tightness of a quantum
observable law. A common hierarchy must retain consistency, positivity, symmetries and
the growth required by the intended reconstruction. Coincidence extensions and the
observable algebra must be specified.

Identification and infrared properties.

A scalar action-density subsector with a running two-point witness is not alone
identification of an interacting Yang–Mills theory. Clustering, vacuum uniqueness and a
mass gap are separate infrared questions. The source draft expressly leaves them open;
our local ultraviolet work does not close them. General color formulas and bare SU(N)
probability lemmas do not extend every upstream SU(2) input to all compact simple
groups.

Efficient audit order.

First freeze the target theory and authoritative companion revision. Verify
normalizations, finite measure identities and domains before long estimates. For our
series, inspect the classical base, strict support norms, common-domain quantum theorem,
finite extraction/ induction, determinant reduction/ comparison, then finite drift
transfer. Audit the full-measure interface independently; the length of the local chain
does not prove it. Historical audit files guide checks but are not independent
certificates.
