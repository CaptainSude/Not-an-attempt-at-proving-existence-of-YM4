   gradient of R times the second fluctuation derivative of the chart.
   Its first nonzero contribution is R2[a,T1(a)[B,C]] at background
   degree two. The other degree-two terms and factorials agree with

   Therefore the exact nonlinear Ward remainder orders (4,3,2)

   Uniform Taylor-derivative estimates require the stated uniform
   exponential classical kernel moments, not just formal zeros.

   sources. Its bubble coefficient is s^2 with one integral over [0,1].
   There is no extra factor two and no cyclic movement of a partial
   trace projector.
4. Matrix exponential differentiation and the difference Duhamel formula
   give all stated estimates without spectral-eigenvector derivatives.
   Their polynomial powers hold even when a simplex heat time is zero.
