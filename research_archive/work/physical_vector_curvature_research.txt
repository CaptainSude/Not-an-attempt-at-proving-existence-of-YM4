1. Actual normalization and inputs

Fix d=4, a compact color algebra of dimension n_g, a fixed primitive odd ell, a composite K=ell^k, and an arbitrary old homogeneous depth M. Work in current bond ANGLES with counting inner products. On a compatible cubic current torus of side n=KN, there are N^4 final K-blocks. Choose one global gauge base as in the physical-quotient determinant identity.

At the flat field, the range of the globally based scalar gradient is precisely the range of the periodic gradient: subtracting the scalar value at the base changes no gradient. Thus the fine physical space is the usual transverse Bloch space, with all harmonic one-forms retained at momentum zero. Write P_f,P_c for the fine/coarse physical projections. The actual operators are
  L=q_M,
  C=C_K,
  A=P_c C|ran(P_f),
  R=L|ran(P_f)-A^T q_(MK) A,
  Q=R+a A^T A.                                      (1.1)
All displayed restrictions are operators on the physical bond space. The equivalent orthonormal-coordinate matrices have the same spectra and traces.

The following inputs have already been proved for the actual flat homogeneous action and ordinary composite average:
 (i) L>=d^T d as a bond quadratic form; on the physical space d^T d is the scalar lattice Laplacian times the identity, apart from its zero modes.
 (ii) q_(MK)>=0, q_(MK) kills coarse gradients, and ||q_(MK)||<=R_q uniformly in M,K and volume.
 (iii) R>=0, and the physical alias Poincare proof gives
       Q>=c_a K^-2 I,                                (1.2)
       c_a=min{B_0^-1,a/(A_0+B_0 R_q)}>0,
       A_0=2c_omega^-2, B_0=(1+2c_omega^-2)/4,
       c_omega=(2/pi)^5.
 (iv) All flat operators are invariant under translation by one final K-block. The physical space and its projection are translation invariant even though the scalar coordinate base is not.
These are Sections 5--6 of contour_heat_comparison_research.txt and the independent physical_quotient_gap_rotation_audit.txt. No scalar pinned or globally based inverse bound is used.

For the rest of this note choose a fixed a>=R_q (a>R_q is harmless). From (1.1),
  Q=L|phys+A^T(aI-q_(MK))A>=L|phys>=Delta_phys.       (1.3)
Combining (1.2),(1.3) gives
  Q >= (1/2)Delta_phys+(c_a/(2K^2))I.                 (1.4)
This inequality is uniform in all the parameters above.

2. Heat traces: use min--max, not operator monotonicity of the exponential

If X>=Y are finite real self-adjoint matrices, their eigenvalues in increasing order obey lambda_j(X)>=lambda_j(Y). Consequently
  Tr exp(-sX)<=Tr exp(-sY), s>=0.
We do NOT infer the generally false operator inequality exp(-sX)<=exp(-sY).

At momentum p in (2pi/n)Z_n^4, put
  P(p)=4 sum_mu sin^2(p_mu/2).
The transverse Maxwell operator has eigenvalue P(p) with multiplicity 3 n_g for p!=0, and zero with multiplicity 4 n_g at p=0. In particular,
  Tr_phys exp(-s Delta_phys/2)
       <=4 n_g sum_p exp(-sP(p)/2).                  (2.1)
Together with (1.4), this gives a total physical trace inequality.

Define the per-final-block heat trace
  H_(M,K,N)(s)=N^-4 Tr_phys exp(-sQ).
Translation by K implies this is also the trace of the ambient compressed heat operator in any one final bond block:
  Tr(P_block P_f exp(-sQ) P_f P_block).
This equality only uses identical diagonal block traces and the finite partition of the current bonds. It does not assert a pointwise min--max comparison.

Let p_n(t)=n^-1 sum_(m mod n) exp[-4t sin^2(pi m/n)]. For representatives |m|<=n/2, sin(pi |m|/n)>=2|m|/n. Gaussian summation then gives, for t>=1,
  p_n(t)<=n^-1+C t^-1/2.
For t<=1, p_n(t)<=1<=sqrt(2)(1+t)^-1/2. Thus, with a fixed C,
  p_n(t)^4<=C[(1+t)^-2+n^-4].                        (2.2)
The identity sum_p exp(-tP(p))=n^4 p_n(t)^4 proves
  H_(M,K,N)(s)
    <=C [K^4(1+s)^-2+N^-4] exp[-c s/K^2],           (2.3)
where c=c_a/2 may be decreased to absorb the factor s/2 in (2.1). Constants depend only on the fixed a,R_q,color dimension and dimension.

The N^-4 term must not be omitted at finite volume. It accounts for the finite number of physical harmonic modes. At s much larger than the square of the fine torus side, the scalar heat trace no longer has a pure s^-2 upper bound.

The same result with the N^-4 term absent holds in the infinite flat structural system:
  H_(M,K,infty)(s)
     <=C K^4(1+s)^-2 exp[-c s/K^2].                  (2.4)
One direct proof is coarse Bloch decomposition: each fiber has finite dimension at most 4 n_g K^4; apply min--max fiber by fiber and integrate over the coarse Brillouin zone. Folding the Maxwell aliases gives K^4 times the ordinary fine scalar heat density. This proof also covers structural flat limits satisfying the same symbols/inequalities, without interchanging varying-background finite determinants.

3. Final physical time and an actual uniform infrared flat bound

Set Qhat=K^2 Q and use final physical heat time u:
  Hhat_(M,K,N)(u)=N^-4 Tr exp(-u Qhat).
Then
  Hhat_(M,K,N)(u)
   <=C [K^4/(1+K^2u)^2+N^-4] exp(-cu),              (3.1)
and the infinite version omits N^-4.

In particular, since K^4/(1+K^2u)^2<=u^-2 for u>0,
  Hhat_(M,K,N)(u)<=C[u^-2+N^-4]exp(-cu).             (3.2)
For u>=1 this is a uniform exponential tail, including all old depths and all compatible volumes. More quantitatively, for every fixed real r and U>=1,
  integral_U^infty u^(r-1) Hhat_(M,K,N)(u)du
     <=C_r(1+N^-4)exp(-cU/2).                       (3.3)
This is an unperturbed heat trace bound. It is NOT a bound on [t^2] of a varying-background trace.

4. Local inverse-power moments

For p>0 the positive spectral calculus gives
  Qhat^-p=(1/Gamma(p)) integral_0^infty
                                  u^(p-1)exp(-uQhat)du.
Its per-final-block trace, finite or infinite, is therefore bounded by (3.1). Splitting the integral at K^-2 and 1 proves
  Tr_block Qhat^-p <= C_p times
      K^(4-2p)+N^-4,         0<p<2;
      1+log K+N^-4,         p=2;
      1+N^-4,               p>2.                    (4.1)
The N^-4 terms are absent in the infinite system. For p>2 the simpler estimate (3.2) immediately gives a constant proportional to Gamma(p-2)/Gamma(p), plus the finite-volume term c^-p N^-4.

These are trace densities, equivalently the appropriate local Schatten moments after compressing the positive spectral measure to a final block. For example Tr(P_block Qhat^-p P_block) has exactly the stated value. The bound does not assert that Qhat^-1 itself is globally trace class in infinite volume, nor identify Tr(P_block Qhat^-1 P_block)^p with the p-th inverse moment. At p=2 the logarithm is an upper bound; no matching lower asymptotic is claimed for the actual operator.

5. A useful exact finite comparison with the un-subtracted physical action

On a finite real ON-SHELL family, so that L D_f=0 and q D_c=0, let
  Q_on=L|phys+aA^TA=Q+A^T q_phys A.
The actual minimum right inverse gives A Q^-1 A^T=a^-1 I. The determinant lemma therefore gives
  det Q_on/det Q=det(I+a^-1 q_phys).                  (5.1)
Since q kills coarse gauge directions, the normalized logarithm of the right side equals N_0 logdet(q+aI) on the full coarse bond space: the missing vertical factor is a fixed power of a. Thus the independently proved uniform Maxwell bound for one half N_0 logdet(q+aI) controls precisely this finite on-shell determinant replacement whenever its Maxwell moment is legitimately defined.

This does NOT turn an arbitrary off-shell family into an on-shell one. The actual general identity retains L=s''-lambda.f'', and globally based projectors have no uniform complex neighborhood. Along the formal affine stationary jets the multiplier is higher order, but passing the separate projected determinants to an infinite affine coefficient is a distinct infrared problem. No such passage is made here.

6. Abstract relative-vertex criterion for an infrared curvature bound

This lemma records precisely one sufficient estimate that would make the flat heat result useful for a second variation. It is conditional at the actual-model interface.

Let X(t)=X+tV_1+t^2V_2+O(t^3) be a real self-adjoint family on a fixed finite-dimensional Hilbert space, with X>0. Assume
  ||X^-1/2 V_j X^-1/2||<=L_j, j=1,2.                 (6.1)
For every u>0,
 |[t^2]Tr exp(-uX(t))|
   <=8(L_2+L_1^2) Tr exp(-uX/4).                     (6.2)
The harmless constant 8 is uniform in dimension and the spectrum.

Proof. The single insertion is -u Tr(V_2 exp(-uX)); its absolute value is at most
  u L_2 Tr(X exp(-uX))
  <=(2/e)L_2 Tr exp(-uX/2).
For the double insertion, cyclic Duhamel or a diagonal basis gives exactly
  (u/2) sum_(i,j) |(V_1)_(ij)|^2
             [exp(-u lambda_i)-exp(-u lambda_j)]
                         /(lambda_j-lambda_i),       (6.3)
where the coincident value is u exp(-u lambda_i). Write V_1=X^1/2 B X^1/2, ||B||<=L_1. For a,b>0,
  ab [e^-ua-e^-ub]/(b-a)
      <=2[a e^-ua/2+b e^-ub/2].                      (6.4)
To check it assume b>=a. If b>=2a, ab/(b-a)<=2a. Otherwise the quotient is at most u e^-ua, while ab<=2a^2 and (ua)e^-ua/2<=2/e. Both cases imply (6.4). Summing row and column squares of B in (6.3) yields at most
  2u L_1^2 Tr(X exp(-uX/2))
    <=(8/e)L_1^2 Tr exp(-uX/4).
This proves (6.2), with room in the constant.

Consequently, if an actual family X(t)=K^2 Q_perp(t) can be identified with a fixed physical space and satisfies (6.1) uniformly in M,K,volume, its normalized second heat coefficient has
  integral_1^infty |N^-4[t^2]Tr exp(-uX(t))|du/u
      <=C(L_2+L_1^2)(1+N^-4).                        (6.5)
The same follows for suitable infinite local limits once the trace/derivative passage has separately been justified. In (6.5), use (3.1) at u/4. No small-u conclusion follows from this argument.

Neither the required uniform identification of the varying physical spaces nor (6.1) is proved for the actual affine source. The fixed-space lemma must not hide derivatives of P_f,P_c or the off-shell constraint/current terms inside an unsupported constant. It is a precise sufficient condition, not a claimed necessary route.

7. Why the curvature problem remains coupled

The new exact flat-holonomy calculation in flat_holonomy_cancellation_research.txt shows that the individual globally based massless scalar factors do not have the same thermodynamic Hessian as their fixed-mass-first counterparts.

For constant commuting final angles t A, the fine angles are t A/K. At fixed K the actual finite identity and local Ward limit force
 lim_(N->infty) N^-4[t^2]logdet Q_perp(tA)
       =C_H |A|^2 (K^2-1) g_4,
 where g_4=integral_BZ [4 sum_mu sin^2(p_mu/2)]^-1 dp/(2pi)^4>0.
The physical determinant therefore carries a growing constant-potential response that cancels the scalar orbit-density response. It cannot be replaced independently by a massive translation-invariant reference merely because its flat spectral gap and heat moments have good bounds.

That calculation is a zero-momentum Ward test, not a magnetic Maxwell coefficient. It is recorded here as the independently derived parent/provenance obstruction, not reproved by Sections 1--6. The main unresolved analytic task remains a coupled local-gauge or jointly regulated comparison preserving this cancellation before any curvature estimates are separated.
