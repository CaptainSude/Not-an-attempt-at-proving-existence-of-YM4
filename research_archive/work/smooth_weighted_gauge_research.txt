1. Definitions and the actual hierarchy

Fix an odd primitive ell>=3 and K=ell^k, k>=1, in dimension d=4
unless another d is displayed. All norms are COUNTING L2 norms at
the indicated lattice level, and all bond coordinates are angles.
Blocks are disjoint centered cubes. Periodic interfaces retain their
usual multiplicity.

Use the actual ordinary references U_j=M_ell^j(U), the actual selected
group-valued internal transports P_j(z,x), and the tangent operators
C_j, Cbar_j, Z_j, B_j of covariant_corrected_scalar_research.txt.
In particular, with D_j phi(e)=phi(e_-)-Ad_Uj(e) phi(e_+),

 T_j(z,x) D_j phi = phi(z)-Ad_Pj(z,x) phi(x),
 Z_j(z)=ell^-d sum_(x in B_ell(z)) T_j(z,x),
 B_j(z,x)=ell^-d Ad_Pj(z,x),
 C_j=Cbar_j+D_(j+1) Z_j,
 Cbar_j D_j=D_(j+1) B_j,
 Z_j D_j=r_ell-B_j.                                      (1.1)

Here T_j(z,x) is the left relative derivative of P_j(z,x), and
r_ell is restriction to the next centers. These are moving-reference
identities, not derivatives of a fixed-identity corrected nonlinear
map. The required contour facts are the actual source facts recorded
in covariant_corrected_scalar_research.txt Sections 1--5: internal
block dependence, endpoint covariance, analyticity and the arithmetic
flat derivative. No single axial contour is substituted for the
selected mean.

Write C^j=C_(j-1)...C_0, A^j=Cbar_(j-1)...Cbar_0.
Then C^j=A^j+D_j z_j, z_0=0. The usual recursion can be rewritten as

 z_(j+1)=B_j z_j+Z_j C^j
         =r_ell z_j+Z_j A^j.                             (1.2)

The second equality is essential: the ordinary tangent has not been
given a false small norm. At flatness A^j is exactly the straight
ANGLE average A_r with r=ell^j.

For a final block B_K(y), write x=x_0,x_1,...,x_k=y for the unique
nested sequence of centers, and
 P_hier(y,x)=P_(k-1)(x_k,x_(k-1))...P_0(x_1,x_0).
All its background dependencies are internal to B_K(y).

2. Arbitrary bounded weights and exact identities

Choose a fixed real profile w_y(x), translated with each final block,
such that

 w_y(x)>=0,  sum_x w_y(x)=1,  w_y(x)<=W K^-d,
 supp w_y subset B_K(y),                                (2.1)

where W is independent of K. Define

 B_w(y,x)=w_y(x) Ad_Phier(y,x),
 Z_w(y)=sum_x w_y(x) (left relative derivative of P_hier(y,x)),
 Cbar_w=C^k-D_k Z_w.                                    (2.2)

Endpoint covariance gives, exactly,

 Z_w D_0=r_K-B_w,      Cbar_w D_0=D_k B_w.               (2.3)

The same simultaneous reference covariance as in (1.1) gives
B_w(U^g)=O_c(g) B_w(U) O_S(g)^T and the analogous Z_w,Cbar_w
identities. Thus this is an admissible auxiliary choice in the
previously proved general tangent determinant bridge.

Disjoint block supports and orthogonality of every Ad(P_hier) give

 B_w B_w^T=(sum_x w_y(x)^2) I=:s_w I.                   (2.4)

This remains exact on the complex bilinear germ. For a common
translated profile, s_w is a background-independent scalar.
Cauchy--Schwarz and (2.1) give K^-d<=s_w<=W K^-d.
Consequently P_w=I-s_w^-1 B_w^T B_w is a local analytic projection,
orthogonal on real fields, and the normalized logdet(B_w B_w^T)
vanishes. No claim of a new nonlinear constraint or pushforward
measure is involved.

3. The exact weighted hierarchical cancellation

Fix a final output y. Define descendant masses at level j by

 w_j(x_j)=sum_(fine x descending from x_j) w_y(x).
Then w_0=w_y, w_k(y)=1 and
 w_(j+1)(z)=sum_(x_j in B_ell(z)) w_j(x_j).

Let P_>(y,z) be the product of the hierarchical transports above the
level-(j+1) site z. It is independent of the descendants below z.
Differentiating an ordered product gives

 Z_w(y)=sum_(j=0..k-1) sum_z Ad_P>(y,z)
           [sum_(x in B_ell(z)) w_j(x) T_j(z,x)] C^j.     (3.1)

Insert C^j=A^j+D_j z_j. The gauge part for fixed j equals

 sum_z Ad_P>(y,z) [
    w_(j+1)(z) z_j(z)
       -sum_x w_j(x) Ad_Pj(z,x) z_j(x)].                  (3.2)

Put
 R_j=sum_(x_j) w_j(x_j) Ad_Phier(y,x_j) z_j(x_j),
where the upper hierarchical product starts at level j. By (1.2),
the first term of (3.2) is R_(j+1) minus
 sum_z w_(j+1)(z) Ad_P>(y,z) Z_j(z) A^j;
the second is R_j. Summation therefore telescopes, with R_0=0
and R_k=z_k(y). Define

 delta w_j(x)=w_j(x)-ell^-d w_(j+1)(parent(x)).
Then

 Z_w(y)-z_k(y)
   =sum_j sum_z Ad_P>(y,z)
           [sum_(x in B_ell(z)) delta w_j(x) T_j(z,x)] A^j.
                                                               (3.3)

For uniform fine weights w=K^-d every delta w_j is zero.
Thus z_k=Z_mean, and (3.3) is the exact formula for Z_w-Z_mean.
It holds at nonflat ordinary references too. In particular the
difference is expressed through PARTIAL CORRECTED products A^j,
not through the potentially larger ordinary products C^j.

4. Uniform flat counting-L2 estimate

At U=1 put r=ell^j. Then A^j=A_r and every upper Ad is identity.
The straight angle average has row absolute sum r and column
absolute sum r^(1-d), with the standard periodic multiplicities.
The Schur estimate gives

 ||A_r||_(2->2)<=r^(1-d/2).                              (4.1)

The descendant bound and the number of descendants give

 |delta w_j(x)|<=2W (r/K)^d,
 ||delta w_j||_2<=2W (r/K)^(d/2).                        (4.2)

The direct sum of all primitive contour maps
 a -> {T_j(z,x)a : x in B_ell(z)}
has norm at most C_T(ell), independent of the number of blocks.
This follows from finite primitive dimension and disjoint internal
block inputs; it is not a direct-K contour estimate. At flatness
the color factor is identity.

For a fixed y, (3.3)--(4.2) bound its j-th row contribution by

 2W C_T (r/K)^(d/2) r^(1-d/2)
              =2W C_T r K^(-d/2).

The fine input edges of the entire scalar row are internal to its
own final block: each primitive internal stage bond has both
descendant blocks in that final block. Different y rows therefore
have disjoint input edge supports. The same row estimate is a
global L2 operator estimate. Summing r=1,ell,...,K/ell gives

 ||Z_w-Z_mean|| <= [2W C_T/(ell-1)] K^(1-d/2).           (4.3)

At flatness Cbar_mean=A_K and ||D_k||<=2 sqrt(d). Hence

 Cbar_w=A_K-D_k(Z_w-Z_mean),
 ||Cbar_w|| <= C_w K^(1-d/2),
 C_w=1+4 sqrt(d) W C_T/(ell-1).                          (4.4)

In d=4 this is C_w/K. The bound is uniform in old classical depth,
volume and the composite depth k, with primitive ell fixed.
No O(K^-1) bound for the full ordinary C^k is asserted.

6. Precisely conditional nonflat counting estimate

The exact formula (3.3) requires no extra quantitative hypothesis.
For a uniform physical-background estimate it is enough to have,
on a specified complex trajectory domain,

 sup_j ||T_j||<=C_T,
 sup_j,y,z ||Ad(P_>(y,z))||<=C_P,
 ||A^j||<=C_A r^(1-d/2), r=ell^j.                       (6.1)

Then the proof of (4.3) applies unchanged, with C_T replaced by
C_P C_T C_A. Own-block support remains exact. Consequently
||Cbar_w||<=C K^(1-d/2), provided the uniform mean-corrected
composite obeys that same bound.

One sufficient actual trajectory input is
 ||log U_j||_infty<=C r epsilon/K
together with the primitive absolute-Schur analytic perturbation
bounds on the same domain. At fixed ell, products of relative
perturbations cost exp(C sum_j r epsilon/K)<=exp(C'epsilon).
This gives both the partial-product bound and the upper transport
bound in (6.1). The existing ordinary-prefix trajectory theorem
provides the relevant small physical-potential class; this note
does not enlarge that class to arbitrary rough link fields or
replace its common-domain requirement by a new assumption of
uniformity. One should cite its precise norm/domain when applying
this sufficient criterion.

On any resulting common physical complex ball, operator Cauchy
gives uniform physical-source derivatives of K Cbar_w, K^2 B_w
and P_w. These are counting operator estimates. They do NOT
bound D P_w derivatives or relative quadratic-form vertices.

7. What the smooth projection repairs exactly

The previous obstruction used Pi onto sharp block constants.
For the new flat X=K^2 Ghat_w, (5.8) removes that obstruction for
ALL bounded smooth physical pure gauges.

Let S be a real self-adjoint multiplication operator with
 ||S||_infty+K||D S||_infty<=L_S.
The discrete product rule and (5.8) give
 ||X^(1/2) S X^(-1/2)||<=C L_S
and its adjoint bound. The same applies to S^2, with constant
C L_S^2. For X(t)=exp(itS)X exp(-itS),

 X'(0)=i[S,X],
 X''(0)=-[S,[S,X]].

Expanding the commutators, and putting the two graph-bounded
multiplications around X in the middle term, proves
 ||X^(-1/2) X'(0) X^(-1/2)||<=C L_S,
 ||X^(-1/2) X''(0) X^(-1/2)||<=C L_S^2,                 (7.1)
uniformly in K. These are genuine raw relative-vertex bounds in
this special, actual gauge-covariant family. The total heat trace
is exactly unchanged, so its first and second variations vanish.
The argument is finite-dimensional on every compatible torus;
compact gauges give the corresponding local infinite identities.

In particular the linear physical phase used in
mean_zero_gauge_vertex_obstruction.txt no longer yields a growing
relative vertex with the smooth weighted projection.

8. Exact remaining issue

The weighted correction solves the full Cbar_w O(K^-1) problem
and gives a better flat scalar energy geometry. It does not yet
give a uniform large-K magnetic heat coefficient for the actual
pair. Operator Cauchy for P_w is not a bound on D P_w.

The hierarchical endpoint transporter may have internal-interface
jumps under a curved background: smooth endpoint weights alone
smooth the OUTER block boundary, not those internal interfaces.
A proof of uniform physical covariant-gradient bounds for the
moving normalized range columns would be an additional input,
and is not inferred here. A follow-up calculation tests this on
affine Cartan curvature. Likewise the old-depth action Hessian
and the residual/current terms still require an actual
cancellation-preserving curvature estimate.

No sign, beta asymptotic, regulator-uniform running-coupling
trajectory, or complete quantum induction is obtained here.
