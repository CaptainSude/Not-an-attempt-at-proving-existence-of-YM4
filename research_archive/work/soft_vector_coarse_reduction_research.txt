8. Uniform actual affine remainder and final reduction

The auxiliary Stilde is covariant under coarse site gauges. Its fixed-block diagonal log density is therefore gauge invariant through the required jets. Consequently

 |beta((1/2)N_0 logdet S)|<=C_S,                          (8.1)

uniformly in old M and K, for the actual joint infinite structural systems and polynomial-jet convention already fixed. Here S means the actual correction: the transfer from Stilde is justified by (6.2), not by off-shell equality.

An equivalent route avoids assigning a Maxwell extraction to arbitrary physical fields: apply (7.5) directly to a smooth physical partition of the affine potential, whose collar C1 norms grow at most linearly in the source block distance, then sum their quadratic products against the exponential source bound at the chosen density root. This bounds its affine density coefficient uniformly. Covariance and cgd give (8.1). The linear source functional vanishes by semisimple conjugation invariance, so nonlinear changes of the physical source representative cannot feed a second-order constant term.
