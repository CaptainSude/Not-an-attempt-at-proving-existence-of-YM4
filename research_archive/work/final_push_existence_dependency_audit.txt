1. Uniformities that must never be conflated

Use M for preceding homogeneous classical refinement, R for the FIXED
elementary composite quantum ratio, J for the FIXED auxiliary grouping,
N for the retained period, b for the fixed small analytic radius, and
g for the running coupling. K may denote a large composite endpoint
ratio in the determinant comparison. These are different limits.

(a) The classical and flat modules give many estimates uniform in
M and compatible volume, on a small global physical-potential ball.
They do not thereby hold on arbitrary mixed-history backgrounds, on
independent free-current domains, or on large-field boundaries.

(b) The local quantum induction fixes R,J and all geometric constants
before choosing b and a weak-coupling ceiling. Its constants can be
very large functions of R,J. Uniformity in the number of iterations
does not imply uniformity as R or J changes.

(c) The local induction works while realized couplings remain weak.
An invariant remainder ball does not alone force this condition.
Its finite continuity modulus need not be volume uniform; shooting
needs only finite-regulator continuity.

(d) The latest spatial/magnetic vector estimates use the chosen
joint infinite structural system, or explicit periodizations of its
exact infinite kernels. Arbitrary actual finite winding kernels are
not identified with those periodizations. Actual finite gaps/flat
heat estimates are separate, weaker facts.

(e) A chosen structural system must include all required old-depth
shifts, endpoint ratios, source derivatives and identities JOINTLY.
One cannot combine a favorable scalar limit from one subsequence
with a vector, reference action, or beta functional from another.

(f) Positive drift for one fixed M, or one selected infinite limit,
does not give the old-depth-uniform finite block bound needed by
shooting. The positivity statement must cover EVERY jointly
obtainable structural limit with one positive margin.

(g) A gap c K^-2 for a constrained/penalized Gaussian is an auxiliary
elliptic gap at the blocking scale. It is not a mass gap for the
reconstructed interacting Yang--Mills Hamiltonian.

(h) Holomorphy in the local connection background A is not the same
as holomorphy in an external observable source w. The latter changes
the density and its counterterms and may break translation symmetry.

separated composite hierarchy alone is not that measure. An
algebraic phase-selection proof or a direct clustering result is
an alternative; neither can be replaced by the words "choose a phase".

9. Nontriviality and Yang--Mills identification

A valid lower and upper action-density two-point estimate, compared
at two explicit separations of the SAME smearing and coupling scale,
can give
 <v,e^-tH v> <=q ||v||^2,  q<1.
Then (1-e^-tH)v is a nonzero positive-energy vector and has zero
projection onto all zero-energy components. Notebook01 gives
explicit compatible bump shapes and a free relative-kernel target.
The interacting witness and its error/limit must still be proved.
This argument proves no spectral gap.

A logarithmically running two-point function does not alone identify
Yang--Mills. The explicit generalized-free spectral countermodel in
work/nontriviality_research.md has the same r^-8/log^2 short-distance
behavior. A Wick square of a generalized free field can additionally
have a nonzero fourth cumulant. Appending an independent constant
mode preserves the ultraviolet witness but destroys unique vacuum.
Thus neither that witness nor "non-Gaussian composite" is an adequate
replacement for identifying the underlying theory.

For a Yang--Mills existence claim in the sense of [JW], construct an
adequate mixed family of renormalized gauge-invariant curvature
polynomials/derivatives (including tensor transformation laws),
their short-distance normalization and mixing, and the quantum-field
axioms. A single scalar named tr F^2 is a useful first sector, not
the full identification. The stress tensor/OPE aspects of the target
also require their own actual operator statements.

If the full Clay target is intended, one must in addition treat every
compact simple gauge group and prove a positive physical mass gap.
Neither an SU(2) scalar-sector limit nor a constrained Gaussian gap
settles those additional requirements.
