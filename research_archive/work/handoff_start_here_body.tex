\section*{What is being handed over}

This bundle contains the current state of an attempt to complete
a rigorous existence argument for a nontrivial four-dimensional
Yang--Mills quantum theory. It includes a detailed research
dossier, an annotated catalogue, all 39 proof notebooks as
PDFs and editable LaTeX, and the underlying research and
internal audit archive.

The central conclusion is precise: the working local ultraviolet
chain reaches a positive actual determinant drift and arbitrarily
long finite weak homogeneous histories. The final notebooks also
establish exact full finite-measure identities and normalized
local Wilson replacement bounds. The complete continuum
existence theorem remains open.

The bundle is intended for a mathematician who can inspect
the argument, challenge its dependencies, and continue at a
specific unresolved theorem. It does not ask that reader
to accept the collection as independently certified.

\section*{The substantial final stopping result}

Notebook 39 finishes a restricted normalized replacement
operation in the \emph{actual bare Wilson measure}.
Take any finite family of vertex-disjoint boxes of
fixed lattice side. Condition on their common exterior,
which includes every boundary edge. Their interior
Wilson laws factor exactly.

On a good boundary, restrict a box law to its
gauge-fixed conditional-minimizer window and divide
by the exact probability of that window. On a bad
boundary, keep the original law. The conditional
tail bound ensures that every divisor is at least
\(1-a_g\), with \(a_g=Ce^{-cT^2}\le1/2\).
The operation preserves the exterior marginal,
gauge invariance and total raw partition mass.
A positive expansion retains every removed
configuration.

An explicit coupling gives, for a selected set
\(A\) of interiors together with the whole exterior,
\[
 \|\mu_{({\rm ext},A)}-\nu_{({\rm ext},A)}\|_{\rm TV}
 =\mathbb E\left[1-\prod_{i\in A}(1-p_i)\right]
 \le |A|a_g.
\]
The convention is \(\|\lambda-\lambda'\|_{\rm TV}
=\sup_E|\lambda(E)-\lambda'(E)|\). Thus a local
comparison does not deteriorate when distant
boxes are added. The theorem also supplies
discrepancy, cluster, entropy, bounded-source
and connected-cumulant estimates.

Its restriction is essential: fixed boxes and
their conditional minimizers are not yet the
nonlinear coarse-average fibers and growing
components of the multiscale construction.
The theorem finishes a normalized local
measure operation, not the existence problem.

\section*{The next theorem}

The first major unresolved step is a
\emph{complete-density transfer theorem}
for the actual chosen renormalization map.
It must match a spatial good-component
integral to the constrained background
and retained coordinates already used,
then bound normalized complementary
components uniformly in their admissible
exterior data, geometry and scale age.

The coupling extraction must belong to
that same full-density construction.
Weak numbers produced by a local
recursion are not automatically the
effective couplings of a different
published large-field theorem.
Ba{\l}aban's complete-density induction
is a serious route once its actual
hypotheses have been matched.

After this come uniform external
observable-source estimates, a common
continuum hierarchy, reflection positivity
and full Euclidean covariance on the
same limit, reconstruction, and a
quantitative nontriviality and
Yang--Mills identification argument.
A physical mass gap is separate.
