# Next construction avenue: a composite ordinary macrostep

17 October 2026. This is an explicitly unproved construction proposal, recorded for a later goal turn. It is not a completed macrostep, a new source identity, or a quantum induction theorem.

## Motivation and the part already available

Keep one primitive odd base ratio ell fixed, for example ell=3, and choose a macro ratio R=ell^s. The proved pre-gauge theorem has constants independent of s at this fixed base. For total refinement MR and the ordinary fine minimizing representative, its partial ordinary average V_p(W) has current link angles and strict supported norm bounded by C_a||w||/R. Consequently a fixed sufficiently large R can give an absolute local-core amplitude margin on a common small background ball. The current obstruction is that the actual physical integral has been matched only for one primitive step, whereas a direct-R ordinary map has not been shown to equal the iterated primitive map.

The proposed repair is to define the macro constraint by the actual composite map

    F_R(V)=M_ell^s(V),

rather than assert equality with a separately defined direct-R average. This is an actual gauge-equivariant group map with a legitimate one-shot pushforward of the original measure. It need not equal s separately gauge-fixed and truncated quantum integrations. The full ordinary classical minimum at refinement MR is already attached to this composite constraint; the exact macro current minimum comparisons and their common domains must nevertheless be written and checked for the selected construction.

## Candidate geometry and the statements requiring proof

1. **One-pivot incidence.** For each final macro bond choose a positive fine/current bond crossing its R-block face on the line of the two macro centers. Since R is a power of the primitive odd ratio, that bond is an interface pivot at each successive primitive scale. The candidate induction is that it affects only the corresponding intermediate output bond and therefore only one final macro equation. Internal rooted contours at each stage should remain within a final R-block and avoid the selected macroface pivot. This must be checked for the exact nonlinear ordinary maps, not just for their flat derivatives.

2. **Flat coefficient.** If that incidence induction holds, its flat angle coefficient should be the product of the primitive pivot coefficients,

       (ell^(1-d))^s=R^(1-d).

The exact coordinate normalization, orientation, primitive endpoint terms and group-mean definitions must be verified. A product of guessed coefficients is not a proof of the nonlinear pivot chart. The desired conclusion is a finite-range analytic macro chart with retained coordinates unchanged and one local invertible pivot equation per macro bond.

3. **Independent rooted gauge.** Choose the actual direct-R internal averaged-contour variable P_R solely as a gauge-fixing device, independently of the composite constraint. Its rooted gauge u(x)=P_R(V_p)(y,x), u(y)=1, should clear those contours. Exact gauge equivariance of F_R means this root-fixed current gauge preserves the final datum W. Internal contours should avoid the macroface pivots, giving T_R h_R=0. These statements require their own verification; no identity between the direct-R average and F_R is proposed.

4. **Gauge insertion and normalization.** Derive the exact gauge-insertion identity, its vacuum normalization, and the actual Haar/pivot factors for this newly selected gauge. Root-fixed gauge integration should preserve the composite constraint, but this does not specify by itself the small-field restriction or its error. The exact integration variables and every retained, contour or reconstructed-field cutoff must be defined. It is not legitimate to inherit a normalization or a rectangular window merely from the formal coordinate identity.

5. **Uniform old-refinement Gaussian theory.** At fixed R the new macro chart has finitely many variables per block, but the classical precision still involves q_M. The required retained flat coercivity, covariance decay, derivative estimates and small-background comparison must be re-established for the composite flat constraint and the independently chosen T_R. Existing uniform-in-M q_M and classical-action inputs are available. Finite local geometry alone does not prove the necessary uniform Gaussian lower bound.

6. **Physical stationary branch.** Complete the common-domain minimum comparisons and the uniqueness argument identifying the rooted representative of V_p as the physical centered branch. Clearing P_R is an explicit gauge operation, whereas physical stationarity and uniqueness use the action comparison and positive reduced Hessian. They must not be conflated. Once this identification is proved, the exact macro integral can be tested against the supported pre-gauge representative and its gauge-invariant composition germ.

## Proposed order after those geometry checks

First fix the primitive base ell and the finite reserves needed for the supported core construction. Its absolute constant C_a is then fixed independently of s. Choose R=ell^s large enough to provide the desired core margin, while retaining the same primitive base in every classical estimate. Construct and audit the actual composite macro chart and Gaussian theorem at that fixed R. Only then apply the auxiliary polymer grouping argument with physical ratio R and choose its separate grouping parameter J. The local-core/small-tail lemma may then reduce the generic D-dependent transport radius loss, with its exact carrier, halo, and tail budgets.

The auxiliary grouping and core/tail arguments do not supply the missing macro chart or Gaussian theorem. Even if all these finite-step interfaces close, the resulting norms still need amplitude control, vacuum/relevant/marginal extraction, running-coupling bounds, large-field estimates and observable domains. The proposal is a concrete next construction route toward a stable small-field domain, not a claim that such a domain or a continuum Yang--Mills theory has been obtained.
