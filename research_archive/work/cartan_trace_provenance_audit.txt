1. Individual matrix and density gauge identities

The left-relative fluctuation transforms linearly by the adjoint at
the initial bond site. The relative target logarithm transforms by
the corresponding adjoint at the initial coarse site. Thus C,
lambda, the full Lagrangian Hessian and the based generator have
the transformation rules stated in gdd-transformations. In particular,
the argument does not discard lambda.f''. The domain of based scalar
fields is unchanged: zero values at centers remain zero under every
pointwise adjoint action.

The fixed scalar completion aI is essential to the displayed simple
covariance. Q_b, G_b and P_c then transform by block-diagonal
orthogonal similarity (complex orthogonal on the analytic germ).
Functional calculus gives the same similarity for their logarithms.
Consequently each diagonal color trace is individually invariant;
this is stronger than invariance of the sum.

The use of the stationary section is legitimate on the local germ:
transforming a minimum at W gives a constrained critical orbit at
W^g, and the previously proved local uniqueness identifies its
selected rooted representative. The intervening fine gauge may
depend on W. This does not affect a pointwise scalar identity under
diagonal conjugation. No globally defined large-gauge section is
asserted.

2. Fixed-endpoint spectral bounds

The path-to-center argument gives a volume-independent positive
based-scalar gap at each fixed K; its crude K^(d+1) Poincare constant
is sufficient and consistent with the K^(-d) upper Rayleigh example.
No mass gap uniform in K is inferred.

For a horizontal vector in ker C, adding D_b T v makes its contour
derivative zero, leaves its Lagrangian value unchanged, and can only
increase its norm. Conversion from retained to current coordinates
uses the fixed-K bound on K_0. This gives the claimed horizontal

The adjusted lift H_b has CH_b=I, D_b^T H_b=0 and

3. Locality of logarithms and their source kernels

Choosing s above the flat upper spectral bound makes I-P(0)/s a
strict contraction. Exponentially weighted Schur bounds make a
small bounded distance-weight conjugation a small operator-norm
perturbation. The complex background ball can then be shrunk so
the conjugated series has a fixed contraction factor r_K<1.

The source-rooted derivative bounds are an actual extra input here,
not a consequence of an operator norm alone. They are supplied by
the strict classical families, the finite-range chart and the
stationary support construction. With these inputs, each
differentiated logarithm series has at most n or n^2 insertions.
Each undecorated power segment has a weighted kernel bounded by
a constant times r_K to its length. Rooted weights for the inserted
vertices join the matrix chain and its sources. Summing the
intermediate coordinates and source labels spends a smaller
exponent; the remaining geometric series with n^2 converges.
This verifies the claimed rooted absolute quadratic-kernel sum.
It is not an unsupported inference from a bound on the extensive
trace or a scalar holomorphic supremum.

4. Affine density equals the total Maxwell moment

For each density, global semisimple color invariance kills its
linear jet in every spatial component. Differentiating its local
gauge identity therefore makes its Hessian annihilate compact
gradients in either slot. Exponential rooted summability extends
this to polynomial gauge parameters. A linear scalar parameter
supplies a constant one-form, giving the individual selected
component's zero row and column sums.

Hence one half of sum k_0(b,c)b_nu c_nu equals minus one quarter
of sum k_0(b,c)(c_nu-b_nu)^2. Translation covariance gives
K(0,r)=sum_x k_0(-x,r-x), and the change of variables
b=-x, c=r-x gives gdd-affine-moment with its exact minus sign and
factor 1/4. All rearrangements have exponential polynomial moments.
Together with the previously fixed beta normalization this yields
beta=2[t^2]rho_0. For mu distinct from nu, midpoint versus tail
coordinates in direction nu introduce no offset.

This is a proof for the specifically constructed invariant
densities, not for an arbitrary representative modulo divergences.
The integration-by-parts counterexample and the conditional use
of the total spatial symmetries are both correctly retained.

6. Polynomial stationary jets and effective S_M

Exponential kernels act on polynomial-growth sequences by absolute
summation. Their inverse identities continue by absolutely
convergent double sums, so the flat retained operator has no
polynomial-growth nullvector. Rooted mixed Taylor bounds absorb
the polynomial inputs at each fixed degree. The orderwise
stationary recursion therefore has a unique polynomial solution,
equal to the extension of the bounded-field analytic jet.

For each fixed finite M, apply this first to the bare minimization
with endpoint M. The explicit affine fine candidate is
unconstrained Wilson stationary, so its whole target multiplier
vanishes. The envelope identity identifies this whole multiplier
with D_left S_M, not just its contraction with the affine path.
That is sufficient to obtain effective-action stationarity in
every current component through order two. The same identity
survives the direct-K rooting by the local gauge identities,
extended coefficientwise with their exponential kernels.

Applying the retained recursion at the next fixed K then gives
the actual stationary jets and lambda_(M,K)=O(t^3).
There is no hidden claim that a one-shot fine inverse is uniform
in M. Uniformity over varying old depths enters only afterward
through the already uniform current-coordinate Taylor kernels
at fixed K. Their polynomial moments pass by exponential
domination, so the structural-limit statement is justified.

7. The actual determinant formula on the probe

Since lambda vanishes through degree two, lambda.f'' contributes
no first or second probe jet. The remaining s'' is still the
Hessian of S_M; it becomes the bare Wilson Hessian only at M=1.
Removing the root gauge from diagonal traces is legitimate by
their individual similarity identity, extended to the same
polynomial jets. The coefficient weights 2,-1,+1 are exactly
twice the original determinant weights 1,-1/2,+1/2.

The fixed-K matrix-log derivatives make these coefficients
well-defined. No positivity at a nonzero infinite magnetic
background, no continuous periodic-flux family, and no value of
the bounded-field analytic map at an unbounded input is used.
