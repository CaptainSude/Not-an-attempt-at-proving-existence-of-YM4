 C_ell^(r)(A_r-D_r b_r)
 =A_(ell r)-D_(ell r)[b_ell^(r)A_r+R_ell b_r].

There is no substitution of a direct-K contour map for the actual

5. The decisive trace calculation

cyclicity identity

6. Scope

The final uniformity is in M, K and compatible regulator
approximations of the specified infinite polynomial jets.
Constants may depend on the fixed primitive ratio ell, chosen
analytic mean, color group, penalty a and inherited classical
kernel bounds. The estimate is not an assertion about arbitrary
finite magnetic-flux families or direct-K ordinary means.
