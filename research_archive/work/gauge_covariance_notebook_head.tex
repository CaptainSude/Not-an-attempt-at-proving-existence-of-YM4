\begin{abstract}
We prove local gauge covariance of the actual homogeneous
small-field chart integral, uniformly in prior classical
refinement. A block-constant lift transports the physical
stationary branch and the full nonlinear retained-coordinate
pivot chart. Its real adjoint action preserves the retained
cutoff exactly. Haar densities, pivot straightening determinants,
the separate delta normalization, the centered action remainder,
and the Gaussian determinant all transform consistently.
The complete generated coarse correction is therefore locally
gauge invariant. Its linear jet vanishes for a semisimple gauge
group and its quadratic jet satisfies the exact finite-regulator
Ward identity. Connected activity bounds imply exponential
Hessian summability. Under an additional translation-invariant
kernel-limit hypothesis the quadratic multiplier vanishes
at zero momentum. The same integral symmetry applies to an
inherited invariant functional when that integral is controlled;
nonvanishing is explicitly required before taking its logarithm.
\end{abstract}
