2. WHAT THE CATALOGUE DOES AND DOES NOT CLAIM

The overall status explicitly denies a complete Yang--Mills existence
claim. Notebook 37 is reported as an actual local homogeneous
leading-coefficient and finite weak-history tuning result under the
series' upstream working inputs. Its scope is not enlarged to the full
Wilson measure. Notebooks 38--39 are identified as positive finite
bare-measure advances with specified domains, rather than as completion
of the multiscale large-field induction.

The limiting Gaussian in Notebook 07 is expressly a free reference.
Notebook 08 retains its flat/source-gauge and regularity qualifications.
Notebook 09 separates the auxiliary current from the nonlinear
constraint-current term added later. The catalogue consistently preserves
the distinctions between:

  * fixed-coupling lattice reflection positivity and continuum covariance;
  * flat/auxiliary Gaussian gaps and a physical Yang--Mills mass gap;
  * global small physical fields and typical fine Gaussian fluctuations;
  * one weighted response estimate and strict simultaneous support norms;
  * a local analytic interaction germ and a complete positive measure;
  * compatible infinite structural systems or explicit periodizations
    and arbitrary actual finite winding kernels;
  * prescribed weak couplings and the exact updated density trajectory.

Notebook 01 retains the conditional angular/all-order and witness
interfaces. Notebook 02 does not claim that its same-limit reflection
positivity proves rotations of a continuum hierarchy. The total Ward and
Maxwell classification in later notebooks is not identified with full
continuum Euclidean covariance. A positive-energy scalar witness is not
reported as a proof of interacting non-Gaussian Yang--Mills identification.

The ending register explicitly leaves H2--H7 open. In particular:
H2 requires the full measure and all mixed/large-field histories, with the
actual coarse-fiber windows and transition/holonomy data; H3 requires
external observables and their renormalization; H4 requires one common
continuum hierarchy; H5 requires the relevant positivity/covariance and
reconstruction for that hierarchy; H6 requires nontriviality and
identification with interacting Yang--Mills; H7 is the separate mass-gap
problem. Nothing in the catalogue accidentally closes these interfaces.

observables. Fixed-box replacement is not presented as growing-component,
post-RG, or averaged-constraint replacement.
