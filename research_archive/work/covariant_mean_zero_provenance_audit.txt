1. Exact finite amplitude (pql)

The all-site injective calculation is legitimate on the indicated real
open set. Fine and target orbit Jacobians are sqrt(det G) and sqrt(det Gc),
respectively. Splitting fine scalar variables into fields zero at final
centers and center parameters has unit Euclidean Jacobian. The contour
gauge integrates only the former; the latter are fixed by the target
delta. Thus the power a^(nc-sc) in I_act^2 is correct.

The physical residual data retain the full constraint-current curvature.
H_res is the Hessian of s-s_eff composed with the actual constraint at a
point with zero first derivative; hence its full gauge null identity is
consistent even when the separate classical actions are not stationary.
No replacement of L by a bare Wilson Hessian is used.

For the mean-zero decomposition, (G^(1/2)V)^T is orthogonal to
Bc=B G^(-1/2) in the needed sense B G^(-1/2)G^(1/2)V=0. Splitting this
vertical space and eliminating its second block leaves precisely the
physical Q_perp. The determinant is
  det QB = a^sc det GB det Gc det(BG^-1 B^T) det Qperp.
Jacobi gives
  det(BG^-1B^T)=det(BB^T) det GB/det G.
Their combination with the actual amplitude has scalar determinant
power ONE:
  I_act = a^(nc/2) det GB sqrt(det BB^T)/sqrt(det QB).
This agrees with the displayed pql result, including normalization.

The auxiliary coupled-matrix shear has determinant one and uses no
inverse of Dc. It is a tangent identity, not an unproved nonlinear
pushforward/Haar change.

The source tangent construction gives BB^T=K^-4 I exactly. The full
scalar completion has complementary eigenvalue K^-2 with constant
multiplicity sc, so it changes the logarithm only by a background
constant. A frame of ker B is not needed in the final local matrices.

The irreducible locus is nonempty arbitrarily near flatness for SU(N):
a regular diagonal cycle holonomy and a second with connected
off-diagonal graph have zero common Lie centralizer. Actual fine
injectivity follows from CD=Dc r and parallel transport. With the
proved mean-zero gaps, holomorphic continuation of the positive
branches proves the identity through flatness. This is a joint
continuation; it does not assign separate globally based determinants
their flat limits. The exclusion of abelian factors is explicit.

2. Spectral estimates (mgs)

Counting normalization checks:

  ||D H_b||^2 <= C_lift K^2 ||Dc b||^2;
  ||D phi||^2 <= 2 C_P K^2 ||P Delta phi||^2
                 +2 C_lift K^2 ||Dc Q0 phi||^2.
The K^4 Parseval ratio in the central-alias lift and the extra K^-2
from one fine difference give exactly the K^2 stated above.
The constant-mode case has zero energy; taking real parts handles
coarse Nyquist representatives without changing constraints.

In the Hodge decomposition the final two estimates produce

             +2C_P K^2||P D^T x||^2+4C_lift K^2||Ax||^2.
The stated transverse alias estimate therefore gives exactly the
displayed C_gap. Harmonic bonds are retained in the transverse term.
No point-pinned gap or scalar global-base inverse is invoked.

The product tent has mean one and mean square at most two. Its internal
and interface derivative counts imply ||DE||<=C1/K, also with periodic
multiplicities. Duality with E gives

Combining this with the gap proves the stated C_Delta and full bond
Laplacian domination.

Heat bounds use eigenvalue min--max, not an invalid matrix

are explicit and correct.

3. Gauge-invariant density and actual Maxwell moment (cgd)

Exact covariance of B,Cbar,D and H_res implies separate covariance of
Ghat and QB in the fine scalar/bond frames. Their diagonal color
traces are individually invariant. After actual stationary substitution,
a change of root gauge is harmless for each diagonal trace, while
background-source dependence is exponential rather than finite range.

For fixed K, the flat lower and upper bounds and uniform old-M source
estimates yield a sufficiently small complex ball and a positive
weighted exponent. Weighted conjugation of the logarithm series, or
a resolvent contour, gives the asserted local source moments.
The series argument must be understood in the conjugated operator
norm with an exponent spare, as written; no raw row norm contraction
is inferred from an unweighted norm contraction.

Each invariant density has zero linear color jet for semisimple color.
Its quadratic Ward identity kills gradients in each slot. Exponential
source moments allow linear scalar parameters; hence its Hessian row
and column sums vanish separately for each bond component. The identity
  [t^2]rho0(t n_nu H)
    = -1/4 sum_(b,c) k0(b,c)(b_nu-c_nu)^2
then follows absolutely. Translation reanchoring identifies this with
half the actual total Maxwell moment. Thus
  beta(D)=2[t^2]rho0(affine probe)
has the correct sign and factor. This avoids assigning a convenient
non-invariant density the total Maxwell coefficient.

The affine source is evaluated only on localized Taylor kernels, not
as a nonzero unbounded argument of a bounded-field germ. The actual
effective S_M Hessian and full residual remain present. The separately
proved actual stationary polynomial-jet identification is the input
needed to use the affine commuting representative.

At small heat time the second Taylor coefficient is O(s); at large
time the fixed-K gap and weighted exponential kernels control polynomial
source insertions by an exponential times a polynomial. Thus the
logarithm identity passes through the two local Taylor derivatives
and the local structural limit. Its sign is
  beta(D)= integral_0^infinity [t^2]Tr0(e^-sQB-2e^-sGhat) ds/s.
This is an exact fixed-K coupled identity, with no residual coarse
determinant. It does not supply K-uniform curvature bounds.
