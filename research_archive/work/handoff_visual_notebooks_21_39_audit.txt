The automated NUL/extraction flags are not established missing-glyph findings; they may arise from LMMathExtension font encoding. This audit does not claim that every flagged glyph on every flagged page was individually enlarged.
3. Notebook 35 page 24 contains only the final bibliography entry. Notebook 39 page 7 contains the closing scope continuation. These are sparse terminal pages with legitimate content, not blank-page or missing-content defects.
4. Notebook 31 page 15 begins with the continuation of a display/prose passage from the preceding page. The viewed sequence shows ordinary pagination, not a lost formula.
5. Typography, page numbering, section structure and link coloring are visually consistent across the assigned notebooks.
