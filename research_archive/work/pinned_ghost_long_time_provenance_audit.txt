1. Sharp flat gap

The Neumann path eigenvalues and cosine amplitudes give
|psi_j(x)|^2<=16/K^4 and lambda_j>=c|j|^2/K^2 in a four-dimensional
K-block. The nonzero-mode sum of |j|^-2 is O(K^2), including the
coordinate faces and axes. Hence the zero-mean point Green
diagonal is O(1), uniformly at every site of the block.

Writing phi=mean+u, the center pin gives mean=-u(center).
Spectral Cauchy-Schwarz controls |u(center)|^2 by internal
energy; the Neumann gap controls ||u||^2 by K^2 times energy.
Thus ||phi||^2<=C K^4 E_internal. Summing blocks uses a subset
of all graph edges, so gives the claimed global lower gap.
The extension from finitely supported fields by closure is
valid, and the compatible periodic version has the same bound.
For K=1 the domain is empty, as explicitly handled.

Together with the previously checked constant-off-centers
test, this is the sharp K^-4 order. The infinite upper bound
can indeed be obtained by cutoff functions on increasingly
many blocks at fixed K; their boundary energy per volume
vanishes. It is not a K^-2 gap.

2. Killed return

The semigroup split has the correct time and square:
  p_s(x,x)=||exp(-sG/2)delta_x||^2
  <=exp(-lambda s/2) p_(s/2)(x,x).
Infinite-lattice killing is dominated by the free walk, whose
return is O(s^-2) for s>=1. This gives
  p_s^K(x,x)<=C s^-2 exp(-c s/K^4).
The retained opening convention makes this an infinite-system
heat bound. It is not justified by replacing a finite-torus
free return probability by its infinite-lattice value.

3. Conditional fourth moment

The exact balanced-count identity E[m^2|return]=s^2 follows by
shifting the factorial series, and E m<=s follows from it.
Given the other bridge Y and m, positive and negative jump
times are independent uniform samples. Their mean terms
cancel, and the independent centered-sum fourth-moment formula
gives C(m+m^2) times the time average of |Y|^4.

Conditional on its own count l, Y(u) is the difference of two
independent binomials. Its fourth moment is bounded by C(l+l^2)
uniformly in u. The coordinate bridges and their counts are
independent after conditioning on return. Therefore
E[Area^4|full return]<=C s^4 for s>=1. Multiplication by the
four-dimensional return probability O(s^-2) gives
E[Area^4 1_return]<=C s^2. No stochastic independence between
area and the pin-avoidance event is needed.

4. Tail and full logarithm

Cauchy-Schwarz combines that fourth moment with killed return:
  E_x[Area^2 1_return-and-avoid]
  <=C s * (s^-2 exp(-c s/K^4))^(1/2)
  <=C exp(-c' s/K^4).
At most K^4 roots cancel the explicit K^-4 curvature factor.
Changing variables s=K^4 v then bounds the integral from K^4
to infinity with ds/s by one K-independent constant.

The exact prefix pinning comparison holds for every upper
time T. At T=K^4 its error is O(1), because h_infinity is
uniformly bounded. The unpinned integral is
4 alpha_g log K+gamma_g+O(K^-4). Adding the bounded actual
tail proves the entire ghost result. Subtracting the audited

In particular the separated ghost tail beginning at K^2
cannot have a uniform O(1) bound.
