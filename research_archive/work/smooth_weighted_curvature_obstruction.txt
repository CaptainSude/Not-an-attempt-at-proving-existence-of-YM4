A CURVED HIERARCHICAL-TRANSPORT OBSTRUCTION SURVIVES SMOOTH WEIGHTS
18 October 2026. Rotation audit research.
This is a proved statement about the actual selected hierarchical
transport construction on a uniformly bounded physical-C1 background
family. It is NOT an assertion that the compactly supported family
below is the globally selected constrained stationary branch.

1. Setting and source convention

Use the actual symmetrized coordinate-order contour mean from the
selected source average. Its derivative at identity is the arithmetic
mean of those contour derivatives. Fix primitive ell odd >=3 and
K=ell^k. The smooth tensor-tent weights, B_w, P_w and basis-free
scalar completion are those of smooth_weighted_gauge_research.txt.

Work on one charged complex root plane with adjoint charge q!=0.
All real statements can equivalently be made on its two-dimensional
real adjoint plane. In a neighborhood containing one final block,
take a Cartan link-angle family
 a_1(t,x)=t x_2/K^2,       a_nu(t,x)=0 for nu!=1.        (1.1)
Extend the physical potential A=K a/t smoothly to zero outside a
fixed number of final blocks. The complete field obeys
 ||A||_infty+K max_nearest|A(x+e)-A(x)|<=C
uniformly in K. Work on a sufficiently large compatible finite torus
so this compact extension is unambiguous.

The construction B_w reads only the original final block. Thus its
first derivative there depends only on (1.1), not on the extension.
At level j, r_j=ell^j, the ordinary background's first Cartan
coefficient is the same Landau field in the rescaled lattice:
 a_(j),1(n)=r_j^2 n_2/K^2.                             (1.2)
Indeed the flat arithmetic average of the primitive root contour
term is zero for this affine field, since centered sums of each
coordinate and of a product of distinct coordinates vanish.
The straight angle average multiplies the physical affine field by
the correct r_j factor. Induction gives (1.2).

For an internal primitive contour from a parent z to child x, the
linear average over coordinate orders integrates the affine Landau
field as the straight-segment expression
 (z_2+x_2)(x_1-z_1)/(2K^2)
when written in original fine coordinates. Among the two relevant
coordinate directions, half of the permutations traverse direction
one before direction two and half after. Other coordinate directions
do not contribute. No nonlinear formula for a group mean on commuting
inputs is assumed; only its actual arithmetic derivative is used.

2. The exact first hierarchical phase

For the nested centers x=x_0,...,x_k=0 of a fine endpoint in the
final block centered at zero, the first hierarchical phase is

 Phi_K(x)=1/(2K^2) sum_(j=0..k-1)
              (x_(j+1),2+x_j,2)(x_j,1-x_(j+1),1).       (2.1)

The normalized range column of B_w(t)^T on the charged plane is
 u(t,x)=exp[-i q t Phi_K(x)+O(t^2)] u(x),
 u(x)=w(x)/sqrt(sum w^2).                              (2.2)
Its norm is one and the row Gram is background independent.
Also |Phi_K(x)|<=C_ell: the total length of the hierarchical route
is at most C_ell K and the affine link coefficient is O(K^-1).

3. A macroscopic phase jump at an internal interface

Put r=K/ell, h_r=(r-1)/2, and consider neighboring endpoints
 x^-=(h_r,x_2,x_3,...),  x^+=(h_r+1,x_2,x_3,...),
with
             15r/16<=x_2<=17r/16.                     (3.1)
Their top child centers have first coordinates 0 and r; their
second top-child coordinate is r. For every j<k, the difference
of the first coordinates of their level-j ancestors is exactly
 delta_j=ell^j, while delta_k=0. Their level-j second coordinates
a_j are equal and satisfy
 |a_j-x_2|<=(ell^j-1)/2,    a_(k-1)=r,    a_k=0.

Subtract (2.1). One obtains the exact formula
 2K^2 [Phi_K(x^+)-Phi_K(x^-)]
   =r^2-(ell-1) sum_(j=0..k-2) ell^j(a_j+a_(j+1))
   =r^2-2x_2(r-1)+E,                                 (3.2)
where
 |E| <=(ell-1)/2 sum_j ell^j(ell^j+ell^(j+1)-2)
       =(r-1)^2/2.                                   (3.3)

For r>=9 and (3.1), (3.2)--(3.3) imply
 2K^2 [Phi_K(x^+)-Phi_K(x^-)]<=-r^2/4.
In particular
 |Phi_K(x^+)-Phi_K(x^-)|>=1/(8 ell^2).                 (3.4)

This is an internal hierarchical-interface jump. It is not a jump
in the original fine connection: its link angles and first physical
differences obey the uniform small-scale bounds in Section1.

4. The normalized range derivative has large physical energy

Restrict the remaining coordinates to |x_nu|<=K/8, nu>=3.
For ell>=3 and all sufficiently large K, both endpoints lie in a
region where every tensor-tent factor is bounded below by a positive
constant. Thus on these edges
 u(x^-)>=c_d K^(-d/2),
 |u(x^+)-u(x^-)|<=C_d K^(-d/2-1).                      (4.1)
There are at least c_ell K^(d-1) such distinct edges.

Let Pi be the flat smooth weighted range projection, P=I-Pi and
 v=u in the chosen color direction. Put
 w=P(Phi_K v)=(Phi_K-c)v,
 c=<v,Phi_K v>.                                       (4.2)
The phase and c are uniformly bounded. Combining (3.4),(4.1) gives,
for all sufficiently large K,
 |w(x^+)-w(x^-)|>=c_ell K^(-d/2).
Consequently
 ||D w||^2>=c_ell/K,       ||w||^2<=C_ell.              (4.3)

The uniform tensor weights repair the outer block boundary but do
not smooth these internal jumps.

5. A growing first relative vertex of the actual scalar completion

Let
 X(t)=K^2 P_w(t) D_U(t)^T D_U(t) P_w(t)+Pi_w(t).
At t=0, write X=X(0). The already proved flat gap gives X>0 and
Xv=v. Differentiating the orthogonal projector and (2.2) gives
 P_w'(0)v=i q w,       Pi_w'(0)v=-i q w.
Since P v=0, all derivatives of D_U(t)^T D_U(t) drop out when
the first derivative of X(t) is applied to v. Exactly,
 X'(0)v=i q (X-I)w.                                   (5.1)
There is no omission of a covariant-Laplacian derivative here:
it is multiplied on the right by P v=0.

It follows that
 ||X^-1/2 X'(0) X^-1/2 v||^2
   =q^2 <w,(X+X^-1-2)w>
   >=q^2[K^2||D w||^2-2||w||^2]
   >=c_ell q^2 K-C_ell q^2.                           (5.2)

Thus the raw first relative-vertex norm grows at least as sqrt(K)
even for the smooth weighted projector, on this uniformly bounded
physical-C1 curved family.

6. Exact conclusion and limitations

The proved O(K^-1) bound for Cbar_w, constant Gram identity,
flat spectral gap, and pure-gauge repair in the preceding note
remain valid. Equation (5.2) shows that they do NOT imply a uniform
relative-form bound for the moving scalar hard projection.

The example uses the actual selected coordinate-order mean at first
order, its actual ordinary hierarchy, and a legitimate small physical
connection. Its compact extension is not shown here to be the chosen
global stationary minimizer for prescribed final W. Therefore this
note does not diagnose failure of a theorem restricted to that branch.
It does refute a uniform raw-vertex assertion on the whole bounded
physical-C1 neighborhood for this particular hierarchical B_w.

The natural remaining options are a genuinely smooth covariant
range construction with controlled internal derivatives, a
cancellation-preserving heat/determinant treatment, or the separate
soft scalar/vector completion. A simple change of endpoint weights
alone does not establish the required curvature comparison.
