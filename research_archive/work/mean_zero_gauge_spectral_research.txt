MEAN-ZERO GAUGE: INDEPENDENT FLAT GAP AND FULL HEAT ESTIMATES
17 October 2026. Rotation audit research. Exact nonlinear determinant bridge is separate.

1. Explicit flat matrices and actual interface

Dimension is four. Current fine sites form the cubic torus of side KN, with K odd, final block centers Ky, and disjoint centered cubes B_y of side K. All scalar/bond coordinates use counting norms. Internal Neumann edges of each cube are counted once; periodic interfaces retain their ordinary multiplicities, including N=1 or 2.

Let D be the full-site forward scalar gradient, Delta_s=D^T D, and d the curl. Let Q0 be the normalized scalar block mean, and
  P=I-Q0^T(Q0 Q0^T)^-1 Q0
the orthogonal projection onto ker Q0. Thus I-P is the block-constant projection. Choose a block-diagonal orthonormal column frame V for ker Q0. Define
  G_B=V^T Delta_s V.
At K=1 its scalar space is empty, so its trace is zero and determinant convention is one.

Let A=A_K be the straight ANGLE average, which is the flat corrected composite differential:
  A D=D_c Q0,       ||A||_(count,2->2)<=K^-1.
For the actual flat old-depth Hessian L=q_M and actual next Hessian q=q_MK, use
  L>=d^T d,  L D=0,  0<=q<=R_q I.
These are established homogeneous classical inputs. With fixed a>=R_q+1 put
  Q_B=L-A^TqA+D P D^T+aA^TA.                         (1.1)
This is the all-site mean-zero-gauge completion proposed in the exact corrected-constraint bridge. The spectral proof below concerns this explicit flat matrix. The nonlinear identity B(1)=Q0 and corrected differential Chat(1)=A is used only to match (1.1) to that bridge; no claim about a nonlinear kernel frame or determinant measure is proved here.

Immediately,
  <x,Q_B x> >= ||dx||^2+||P D^T x||^2+||Ax||^2.       (1.2)
We will prove that the right side controls K^-2||x||^2 uniformly.

2. Scalar block Poincare and a harmonic block-mean lift

If Q0 psi=0, the Neumann cube Poincare inequality, summed over blocks, gives
  ||psi||^2<=C_P K^2 ||D psi||^2.                     (2.1)
One may take C_P=1: the first nonzero cube eigenvalue is
4 sin^2(pi/(2K))>=4/K^2, and internal edges are a subset of all edges. The K=1 case is vacuous. Therefore
  G_B>=C_P^-1 K^-2 I.                                (2.2)

For coarse scalar b, let H_b minimize ||D phi||^2 subject Q0 phi=b. The constraint is onto and fixes the constant nullspace, so the finite minimizer is unique. Its Euler equation is
  P Delta_s H_b=0.
There is a scale-uniform upper bound
  ||D H_b||^2<=C_lift K^2 ||D_c b||^2,
  C_lift=(pi^2/4)(pi/2)^8.                            (2.3)

Here is the normalization proof. For a coarse frequency p in [-pi,pi]^4, use only the central fine frequency p/K. Scalar averaging has multiplier
  a0(p)=product_mu [sin(p_mu/2)/(K sin(p_mu/(2K)))],
up to the harmless centered-block phase, and |a0|>=(2/pi)^4. Divide the lifted amplitude by a0. Its fine gradient satisfies
  |e^(ip_mu/K)-1| <= (pi/(2K)) |e^(ip_mu)-1|.
Counting Parseval multiplies a squared fine amplitude by K^4 relative to the coarse one. Thus the lifted energy is at most C_lift K^2 times the coarse gradient energy. At p=0 the lift is constant and has zero energy. The selected central Fourier frequencies are legitimate on every KN-periodic torus. For real b one may take the real part of a complex feasible lift, which preserves the constraint and does not increase the energy, including coarse Nyquist modes.

For arbitrary phi, put b=Q0 phi and psi=phi-H_b. Then Q0 psi=0 and
  P Delta_s psi=P Delta_s phi=:g.
The Euler equation gives ||D psi||^2=<psi,g>. Applying (2.1) yields
  ||D psi||<=sqrt(C_P)K||g||.
Consequently,
  ||D phi||^2
   <=2C_P K^2||P Delta_s phi||^2
                         +2C_lift K^2||D_c Q0 phi||^2. (2.4)

3. Independent full vector gap

Use the orthogonal periodic Hodge decomposition
  x=x_T+D phi,       D^T x_T=0.
The harmonic bond modes belong to x_T. Choose phi with zero global mean; its constant is irrelevant.

The already proved transverse alias estimate for the straight average is
  ||x_T||^2 <= K^2[A0||P_c A x_T||^2+B0||d x_T||^2],
  A0=2c_omega^-2, B0=(1+2c_omega^-2)/4,
  c_omega=(2/pi)^5.                                   (3.1)
This is a Maxwell/averaging estimate, so its constants are independent of old depth. It includes zero momentum without dividing by it.

Since A D phi=D_c Q0 phi,
  P_c A x_T=P_c Ax,
  ||D_c Q0 phi||^2<=2||Ax||^2+2K^-2||x_T||^2.
Also P Delta_s phi=P D^T x and d x_T=dx. Substituting in (2.4) and adding ||x_T||^2 proves
  ||x||^2<=C_gap K^2[
                ||dx||^2+||P D^T x||^2+||Ax||^2],       (3.2)
where one valid constant is
  C_gap=max{(1+4C_lift)B0,
            2C_P,
            (1+4C_lift)A0+4C_lift}.
Together with (1.2),
  Q_B>=C_gap^-1 K^-2 I.                              (3.3)

This proof uses the corrected flat A, whose full counting-L2 norm is K^-1. It does not apply that estimate to the ordinary unprojected C_K. It uses neither a point-pinned scalar gap nor a volume-dependent scalar inverse.

4. A local tent lift and full Laplacian domination

A useful strengthening controls the full bond Laplacian, not just the gap. Let Pi=I-P be the block-constant scalar projection. There is a scalar map E:ran Pi->all sites with
  Pi E=I,       ||E||<=C_0,       ||D E||<=C_1/K,       (4.1)
where C_0=2^(d/2), and C_1^2=80 d 2^(d-1) is a conservative choice.

For K=2h+1>=3 define
  f(r)=K/(h+1) [1-|r|/(h+1)],  -h<=r<=h.
Its mean is one, its mean square is at most two, its endpoint value and adjacent difference are at most 4/K. If e is constant c_y on B_y, set
  (Ee)(Ky+r)=c_y product_mu f(r_mu).
The block mean is c_y, and the L2 bound follows by tensorization. For a direction mu, internal edges contribute at most
  16 2^(d-1) K^(d-2) sum_y |c_y|^2;
interfaces contribute at most
  64 2^(d-1) K^(d-3) sum_y |c_y|^2,
using sum_y|c_(y+e_mu)-c_y|^2<=4 sum_y|c_y|^2.
Since ||e||^2=K^d sum_y|c_y|^2, summing directions proves (4.1). All periodic repeated interfaces are counted normally. At K=1 take E=I; the same conservative constants apply.

For any bond field x and e in ran Pi,
  <Pi D^T x,e>
      =<x,D Ee>-<P D^T x,Ee-e>.
Thus
  ||Pi D^T x||
      <=(C_1/K)||x||+(C_0+1)||P D^T x||.             (4.2)
This is the key local duality estimate; no inverse Laplacian is used.

The full bond Laplacian obeys
  <x,Delta_1 x>=||dx||^2+||D^T x||^2.
From (4.2),
  <x,Delta_1 x>
   <= ||dx||^2+[1+2(C_0+1)^2]||P D^T x||^2
                                      +2C_1^2 K^-2||x||^2.
Use (1.2),(3.3) to obtain
  Delta_1 <= C_Delta Q_B,                             (4.3)
  C_Delta=1+2(C_0+1)^2+2C_1^2 C_gap.
Consequently
  Q_B >= (1/(2C_Delta))Delta_1
                          +(1/(2C_gap K^2))I.         (4.4)

5. Heat traces and inverse moments for BOTH mean-zero-gauge factors

Let O be Q_B or G_B, and use the trace per final block. For Q_B this is the ordinary bond trace; for G_B it is the trace in the block-local ker Q0 frame. All operators are K-translation invariant.

For Q_B, (4.4) and finite-dimensional eigenvalue min--max bound its total heat trace by the scalar lattice heat trace with a fixed rescaling of time, times 4 dim(g), and exp[-c s/K^2].

For G_B use (2.2):
  G_B >= (1/2)G_B+(1/(2C_P K^2))I.
The eigenvalues of the compression V^T Delta_s V dominate the correspondingly indexed eigenvalues of the full scalar Delta_s, by the variational principle. Thus its heat trace is bounded by the full scalar heat trace. Together with the displayed gap this proves the same heat bound.

In final time u,
  N^-4 Tr exp(-u K^2 O)
    <=C[K^4/(1+K^2u)^2+N^-4]exp(-cu).                (5.1)
Infinite flat structural systems omit N^-4. One can obtain their bound directly by coarse Bloch integration, avoiding any nonflat determinant limit.

Mellin integration gives exactly the local inverse-moment bounds
  Tr_block(K^2 O)^-p <= C_p times
       K^(4-2p)+N^-4,   0<p<2;
       1+log K+N^-4,   p=2;
       1+N^-4,         p>2.                          (5.2)
The infinite versions omit the finite-volume term. Every weighted final-time heat tail over u>=1 is exponentially integrable uniformly.

The basis-free scalar completion used in the exact analytic determinant bridge is
  Ghat=P Delta_s P+K^-2(I-P).
At flatness its two scalar subspaces reduce it. Its normalized final-time heat density is exactly that of G_B plus dim(g) exp(-u), because the complement has dim(g) coordinates per final block. This additional term obeys (5.1) after decreasing c: for u<=1, K^4/(1+K^2u)^2>=1/4; for u>=1 it is at least 1/(4u^2), and u^2 exp[-(1-c)u] is bounded if c<1. Its p-th normalized inverse moment adds exactly dim(g), which is absorbed in every case of (5.2). Hence Ghat and Q_B, the actual basis-free pair, obey the same estimates.
