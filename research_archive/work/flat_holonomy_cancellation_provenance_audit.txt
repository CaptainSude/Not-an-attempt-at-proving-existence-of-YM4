holonomies equal to the identity. Every short path representing the
same displacement has the same commuting transporter, including paths
in a lifted boundary block. Thus each ordinary blocking stage produces

The actual selected mean is evaluated on identical transporters, so
no stronger arbitrary-torus arithmetic property of the mean is needed.

The same construction at all previous fine levels gives a bare
Wilson field of zero action. The real Wilson action is nonnegative,
so the current and final classical minimum values are zero.
After the actual local root gauge, the field is in the selected
small stationary chart. Uniqueness of that local minimum germ
identifies it with the actual branch. This is a fixed finite-germ

are unchanged by the root gauge. Their spectra can be computed in
