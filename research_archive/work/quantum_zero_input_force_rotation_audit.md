# Independent audit of the fixed-outer-radius quantum force

17 October 2026. Auditor: rotation_audit. Read quantum_zero_input_force_research.md Sections1--4 and6 against the common-domain and quantum-local-limit bounds. No outputs edited or compilation performed.

The quadratic-force result passes. Fixing the outer radius first is substantive: the chart, threshold, covariance and logarithm branch must be those of the same actual zero-input map when it is restricted to b. The note explicitly supplies this compatibility and uses the same joint classical system.

The outer estimate is an anchored coefficient bound, not an extensive action estimate. The global adjoint invariance of the total kills its first derivative slot by slot for a semisimple Lie algebra. Individual coefficients need not be invariant or have zero linear terms. Removing their degree-zero and degree-one parts changes neither the normalized total nor its quotient class.

The phase projection estimate on a balanced polydisc has no coordinate-count factor. Summing the geometric degree tail separately for each coefficient and then the rooted carrier sum gives the stated 2H_* b²/rho² bound without interchanging a root supremum and an order sum. The b-independent reference projection and the beta b^(-2) norm therefore give the stated O(b²) extracted force and O(1) Maxwell coefficient.

The finite-block envelope calculation in Section4 is correct: K^k r+C_0 b² sum_(l<k)K^l bounds every intermediate stage, and the Duhamel contraction plus force bounds preserve r=2C_0 L_q b². If C_0=0 this radius is zero; a nontrivial radius can instead be chosen with coefficient max(1,2C_0 L_q). This is an optional harmless convention, not an error.

One comparison-budget fidelity correction was sent to the author: (4.5) used eta_*/(32e), whereas the cited two-input qll Lipschitz construction uses eta_*/(128e). Imposing the latter stronger budget, as requested, costs only a further final decrease of g and changes no conclusion.

The interpretation is properly restricted. A same-coupling amplitude comparison does not control coupled trajectories with different running couplings. O(1) one-step inverse-coupling increments give no sign or control of an infinite drift. An infinite quotient envelope does not automatically construct uniformly bounded actual finite-regulator histories.

The optional fixed-threshold parity refinement in Section5 was not part of this audit.
