1. Actual operator and normalization

In a charge-q color sector, D_b^T D_b is 8I minus magnetic adjacency
on noncenter sites. The diagonal remains 8 next to a pin: this is
Dirichlet killing, not a reflected walk with reduced degree.
The formal current-link probe is exp(i t x_nu H/K^2). On a closed
walk its phase is exp(i q t Area/K^2), independent of the starting
point. The quadratic coefficient of this phase is
-q^2 Area^2/(2K^4). Multiplication by the definition -2[t^2] gives
the positive factor q^2/K^4. Summing charge multiplicities produces
C_H, with no further real-versus-complex factor.

The output block has K^4 residue classes, one of which is pinned.
The unpinned comparison uses all K^4; their count cancels K^-4.
Thus the stated unpinned h_infinity has exactly the final-coordinate
normalization of the actual ghost term. In particular its limiting
constant is alpha_g=C_H/(48 pi^2), and integrating to K^2 gives
2 alpha_g log K=C_H log K/(24 pi^2).

The actual stationary section has the same first and second probe
jets as this explicit rooted affine candidate for every old M.
Based gauge conjugation leaves the block diagonal color trace
unchanged. Since the quadratic heat coefficient depends only on
those two operator jets, the resulting h_K is the actual one for
every M and every compatible structural limit. No M-dependent
microscopic-coordinate count or time rescaling is introduced.

2. Exact bridge-area calculation

For one coordinate, conditioned on return, the positive and negative
Poisson counts equal m and their times are independent uniform
samples. The conditional mean count is s I_1(2s)/I_0(2s).
At fixed m the signed point process has diagonal second-moment
mass 2m du/s and off-diagonal density -2m du dv/s^2. Integrating
gives the stated bridge covariance and v_s=2 I_1/I_0.

Coordinates remain independent after conditioning each to return.
There are almost surely no simultaneous jumps in distinct axes.
The area variance is therefore v_s^2 times
  integral_0^s (u-u^2/s) du
  -s^-1 integral_0^s integral_0^s (min(u,v)-uv/s) du dv
which equals v_s^2 s^2/12. Multiplication by the four-coordinate
return probability proves
  h_infinity=(C_H/3) s^2 e^-8s I_0(2s)^2 I_1(2s)^2.
As a separate normalization check, its small-s term is C_H s^4/3,
matching the eight oriented unit-area length-four loops divided
by 4!.

4. Pinning comparison

For a length-n closed walk with n>=1, list its n cyclic vertices,
including the starting vertex but excluding its duplicate endpoint.
Each vertex excludes at most one starting residue modulo K.
Therefore r_K<=min(n,K^4), including the residue with a pinned root.
Area is invariant under translation of a closed walk.

The difference of the unpinned and killed block traces is thus
exactly the nonnegative generating series written in the fragment.
Replacing r_K by n gives
  0<=h_infinity-h_K
    <=K^-4 (s h_infinity'+8s h_infinity).
The right side is nonnegative because it is itself a generating
series with nonnegative coefficients; no sign of h_infinity'
is assumed.

Integrating with ds/s gives
  K^-4 {h_infinity(T)+8 integral_0^T h_infinity(s) ds}.
At T=K^2 this is O(K^-2), uniformly in K>=1.
The zero-length walk has zero area, so it causes no exception.
At K=1 the based space is empty and the bounds remain harmless.

5. Fixed finite part and intermediate band

The definition of gamma_g is absolutely convergent. The tail of
(h_infinity-alpha_g)/s beyond K^2 is O(K^-2). Adding the pinning
estimate gives the precise prefix law in the theorem, with one
K-independent finite part gamma_g.

For s>=1, the pinning bound is O(s/K^4), while the unpinned error
is O(1/s). At s=K^2 u this is exactly
  O((K^2 u)^-1+u/K^2), K^-2<=u<=1.
The factors and the comparison to a vector contribution of
10 alpha_g in the specified reference convention are consistent.

6. Scope and document fidelity

The walk shapes in this proof are closed in Z^4. A finite periodic
trace also contains winding walks, and a nonzero affine magnetic
family is not a periodic analytic family through zero. Thus this
proof is for the infinite structural-system coefficient obtained
after the earlier fixed-K local limit, not a uniform finite-torus
prefix assertion. The requested opening clarification makes this
explicit.

The fragment proves a heat-time prefix through K^2, not the entire
ghost determinant. In particular it does not bound the integral
after K^2, infer a full determinant sign or establish the coupled
vector/ghost drift. Its error constant is genuinely independent
of M and of the structural limit because the relevant ghost jets
are identical there.
