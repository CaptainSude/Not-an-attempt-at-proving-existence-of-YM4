PASS. The example is an actual compact pure-gauge conjugation of the
covariant scalar completion, on a finite compatible torus large enough
for the fixed physical cutoff neighborhood.

For v=K^-2 1_B, its counting norm is one and Xv=v. The centered affine
phase gives w=PSv=Sv and
  ||w||^2=q^2(K^2-1)/(12K^2).
The two normal faces contribute
  ||Dw||^2 >= 2q^2 h^2/K^3,
so <w,Xw> >= 2q^2 h^2/K. Consequently the squared relative first
vertex applied to v is
  <w,(X^-1+X-2)w> >= 2q^2 h^2/K-q^2/6.
Its norm grows at least c|q|sqrt(K).

The extension of S is bounded in physical coordinates and has fine
first difference O(1/K). It therefore does not exploit an unbounded
gauge or an infinite-volume trace. The eigenvalues and total heat trace
remain constant under the exact unitary conjugation. This correctly
demonstrates cancellation between the second vertex and the two-first-
vertex term, not a failure of the mean-zero determinant or its gap.

It refutes uniform raw relative-vertex bounds over all these gauges.
It does not refute a magnetic moving-frame estimate or a bound for the
combined gauge-invariant curvature trace.
