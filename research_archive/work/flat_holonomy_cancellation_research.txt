EXACT FLAT-HOLONOMY CANCELLATION IN THE PHYSICAL DETERMINANT

Purpose: quantify the compensation forced by the global-base scalar
defect. This is a zero-momentum Ward test, not a Maxwell coefficient
calculation and not a bound on nonzero-curvature response.

1. Scalar defect (independent derivation of provenance's observation).
On a periodic n^4 lattice, let one charge q see constant link phase
theta=t q A in direction mu. The unbased scalar spectrum is
 lambda_p(t)=2 sum_nu [1-cos(p_nu+t q A delta_mu,nu)].
Set V=n^4 and remove one site's row/column to form G_n(t).
For t nonzero and away from accidental zero modes, Jacobi and Fourier
diagonals give
 det G_n(t)=V^-1 prod_(p!=0)lambda_p(t)
           [1+lambda_0(t) sum_(p!=0)1/lambda_p(t)].
Both sides continue analytically through t=0. Since
lambda_0(t)=q²A²t²+O(t^4),

 [t²]logdet G_n(t)
 =q²A² sum_(p!=0) [
       cos(p_mu)/lambda_p(0)
       -2 sin²(p_mu)/lambda_p(0)^2
       +1/lambda_p(0)].

The first two terms are the partial derivative
 partial_(p_mu)[sin(p_mu)/lambda_p(0)].
They have O(|p|^-2) singularity, integrable in four dimensions. Their
Brillouin-zone integral vanishes by integration by parts after removing
a radius-epsilon ball: its boundary flux is O(epsilon²).
Their Riemann sums converge: the grid contribution from radius epsilon
is bounded by C n^-4 sum_(0<|m|<=Cn epsilon) (|m|/n)^-2
<=C epsilon², with a harmless O(n^-2) edge adjustment.
The same argument applies to 1/lambda.
Consequently

 lim_(n->infinity) V^-1[t²]logdet G_n(t)
       =q² A² g_4,
 g_4=integral_[-pi,pi]^4 dp/(2pi)^4 [2 sum_nu(1-cos p_nu)]^-1>0.

Summing charges gives C_H A² g_4. Neutral charges contribute zero.
For a vector A of commuting constant link angles the answer is
C_H |A|² g_4; off-diagonal terms vanish by coordinate reflections.

At each fixed scalar mass m>0, the corresponding normalized full
logdet coefficient is the integral of a periodic momentum derivative
and vanishes. The principal-minor correction is rank dim(g); its
fixed-m derivatives are O(1), so its normalized contribution vanishes.
Thus taking the thermodynamic limit before mass removal gives zero.
The massless globally based limit gives the nonzero value above.

2. Fine/coarse normalization.
Fix K=ell^k and old depth M. Final period N; fine current period KN.
Use actual final constant links W_mu(t)=exp(i t A_mu H), all commuting,
and current links U_mu(t)=exp(i t A_mu H/K).
Each ordinary blocking stage preserves constant commuting connections,
so F_K(U)=W exactly. These configurations have zero plaquette action.
The rooted gauge representative is obtained by actual contour gauge;
gauge conjugation leaves Gf, Gc determinants unchanged. The local
minimum germ identifies these flat representatives with the actual
stationary branches. This uses the fixed finite M,K,N germ only.

The fine scalar determinant is evaluated at current angle t A/K,
with (KN)^4 sites, while the coarse determinant has angle t A and N^4
sites. Therefore

 lim_(N->infinity) N^-4 [t²](logdet Gf(t)-logdet Gc(t))
       = C_H |A|² (K²-1) g_4.                    (2.1)

This holds for fixed K, with every actual old M (the generators on
this branch are the same covariant scalar difference operators).
It is not the magnetic heat coefficient alpha_g logK.

3. The actual total has zero thermodynamic constant-flat response.
At fixed M,K, Notebook33 gives the actual leading correction D_N a
translation-invariant Hessian kernel with common exponential row and
source bounds, plus local gauge Ward identities on its infinite limits.
For any structural limit, each localized density is gauge invariant
and its quadratic kernel kills lattice gradients. The Ward identity
extends to polynomial scalar gauge parameters by absolute exponential
summability. The constant field A is the gradient of the linear scalar
parameter x -> A.x (up to the generator sign). Hence the infinite total
quadratic symbol vanishes at momentum zero.

Equivalently, for the finite coefficients,
 N^-4[t²]D_N(W(t))=1/2 sum_(orientation,color; displacements)
           K_N(0,r)[A,A].
Here the orientation and color contractions are finite. The exponential
row bound makes the tail uniformly small. On each local-limit
subsequence the finite central sum converges and its full limit is zero
by the Ward identity. Compactness and uniqueness of this scalar
subsequential value imply

 lim_(N->infinity) N^-4[t²]D_N(W(t))=0.          (3.1)

No derivative of a nonperiodic affine flux family on a torus is used.
W(t) is an exactly periodic, genuinely flat holonomy family.
No assertion that D_N is identically zero at finite N is needed.
The locality constants may depend on K; K is fixed before N->infinity.
The same subsequence argument is uniform in old M when using the
Notebook33 uniform old-depth fixed-K bounds and all structural limits.

4. Forced physical-precision compensation.
The exact finite identity in Notebook34 is
 2 D_N=N0(logdet Gf-logdet Gc-logdet Qperp).
Subtract (3.1) from (2.1), with identical N, K, branch and base.
This proves

 lim_(N->infinity) N^-4[t²]logdet Qperp(W(t))
       = C_H |A|² (K²-1) g_4.                  (4.1)

Thus the physical determinant has a quadratically growing constant-
connection response even though Qperp(0)>=cK^-2 on the physical space.
The corresponding contribution -1/2 logdet Qperp cancels the scalar
orbit ratio exactly at zero momentum. The identity fixes its sign.
