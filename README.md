# Not an attempt at proving existence of YM4

A continuation of the public Yang–Mills research released in [ethanwsussman/Yang--Mills-LLM-experiment](https://github.com/ethanwsussman/Yang--Mills-LLM-experiment), by Levent and his collaborators and hosted by Ethan Sussman.

The complete research handoff is being uploaded: 39 notebooks, 3 reading documents, editable sources, and the research archive. The full guide and open-problem map will appear with that upload.

This continuation does not yet claim a completed four-dimensional continuum existence proof or a mass-gap proof.
